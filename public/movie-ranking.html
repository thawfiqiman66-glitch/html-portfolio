The Best Movie according to Thawfiq Iman

My Top 3 Movies of all time

1. The Shawshank Redemption

This film is a masterpiece of storytelling, showcasing the power of hope and friendship in the darkest of circumstances.

2. The Godfather

A gripping tale of power, corruption, and family loyalty set against the backdrop of the Italian-American mafia.

3. Pulp Fiction

Quentin Tarantino's non-linear narrative and memorable characters create a unique cinematic experience that has influenced countless filmmakers.
